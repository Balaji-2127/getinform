Here's a quick tour of Adonmo. In the next few minutes, you'll see everything you need. From a client's spreadsheet, to a live map they can actually explore.

[02]  02-dashboard.wav
This is where you land. Your campaigns, your clients, and the size of your whole inventory, all at a glance. Your most recent campaigns sit just below, so you can pick one straight back up.

[03]  03-upload.wav
To start a campaign, click Upload campaign sheet. Give it the client's name and a campaign name. That's what your client will see. Then attach your shortlist. It's the master sheet you already use, with the properties you're not pitching removed, and one tab per city.

[04]  04-submit.wav
Click upload. Every row is checked against your live inventory, so only real, available properties make it in.

[05]  05-map.wav
And here it is. Your client's properties in blue, on a real 3D map, with the rest of your inventory in green so they can see what else is nearby. The totals up top are the numbers clients always ask for. Screens, households, and monthly budget.

[06]  06-tour.wav
On a call, press Tour the shortlist. It moves from one property to the next and stops on each one, so you can simply talk your client through it.

[07]  07-edit.wav
If the client wants changes, you don't need a new sheet. Open Edit shortlist, remove anything they don't want, search for anything they'd like to add, and save. The map updates straight away.

[08]  08-update.wav
And when they want to expand into more cities, use Update sheet. Upload the new version and choose Add to this campaign. Everything you had stays, and the new cities come in. It's still the same campaign, and the same link.

[09]  09-share.wav
When it's ready, copy the client link and send it over. They don't need a login or an app. This is exactly what they'll see, with their own branding on it.

[10]  10-inventory.wav
Back in the app, Inventory lets you explore everything you have, city by city. Search for a property, check its numbers, see which clients already have it, and add it straight to any campaign.

[11]  11-reports.wav
Reports keeps every campaign in one place. You can export them to a spreadsheet, open any of them again, or remove the ones you no longer need.

[12]  12-close.wav
And if you ever need a refresher, this video and answers to the most common questions are always under Help and FAQs. That's it. You're ready to build your first campaign.
